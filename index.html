Meet Emily

About

Hi, I'm Emily — a dedicated, compassionate pet sitter based in Northern Colorado, with over five years of experience caring for dogs, cats, reptiles, small animals, and even the occasional farm friend. I've always had a deep connection with animals, and that bond has only grown stronger through years of hands-on care and training.

Whether you're heading out for the day or away for a week, I provide thoughtful, reliable care that helps your pets feel safe and secure in their own space. I've taken classes in dog training, and I'm comfortable with a wide range of needs — from leash walks and medication routines to cleaning up after little messes and tuning into an animal's mood and comfort level.

I'm equally at home playing with a high-energy pup as I am calmly bonding with a shy cat or handling the unique needs of reptiles and rodents. Animals deserve presence, patience, and kindness — and I bring that to every visit.

When I care for your pets, I treat them like family.
